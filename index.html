The Atelier Standard

Where architectural clarity meets human expression.

Founded on the belief that eyewear is the most prominent element of personal architecture, STACKLY merges time-honored artisanal fabrication with contemporary minimalist design.

Every frame passes through eighty-four individual manual steps in our workshops: from diamond-wire contour cutting of Italian Mazzucchelli acetate blocks to 72 hours of Japanese beechwood tumbling for unmatched organic luster.

Zero Petroleum

Certified bio-acetate derived exclusively from cotton seeds and wood pulp.

Fukui Titanium

Cold-forged in Sabae, Japan for hypoallergenic strength and featherweight balance.
