White-Glove Optical Services

From private video face-mapping to bespoke Zeiss progressive lens fittings, experience the elevated care of a traditional European optical maison.

Service 01 · Private Session

One-on-One Optical Consultation

Choosing eyewear is an intimate architectural endeavor. Our certified opticians conduct comprehensive 45-minute virtual or private salon consultations to understand your visual routine, lighting environments, and aesthetic aspirations.

We evaluate nasal bridge angles, pupillary distance, and cheekbone prominence to shortlist frames that harmonize seamlessly with your personal style.

Book Consultation Appointment
Service 02 · Morphological Analysis

Digital Frame Styling & Face Mapping

Utilizing high-precision digital photogrammetry, our stylists map sixty-eight cranial anatomical points. We calculate whether your face requires an upward-swept browline, an architectural polygon, or a low-bridge keyhole design.

  • Oval, round, square, and diamond silhouette harmonizing
  • Complexion analysis for acetate palette coordination
  • Digital try-on lookbook prepared within 24 hours
Request Digital Lookbook
Service 03 · Optical Sciences

Precision Lens Selection Guidance

A frame is only as magnificent as the clarity it yields. We partner with world-renowned German optics laboratories to offer bespoke high-index 1.67 and 1.74 ultra-thin mineral glass and organic CR-39 lenses.

Whether you require blue-light harmonic filtering for intense studio monitor exposure, drive-safe polarizing filters, or photochromic transitions, our optical team formulates the exact prescription stack.

Service 05 · Longevity & Restoration

Atelier Care, Polishing & Ultrasonic Cleansing

Like fine chronometers, luxury eyewear requires occasional care to maintain its structural poise. Over years of wear, skin lipids can gently dry acetate surface moisture.

We provide complimentary lifetime ultrasonic cleaning, Japanese hinge screw recalibration, and re-polishing at any of our flagship salons. Your frames will return with factory-fresh luster and hinge resistance.
