Frequently Addressed Eyewear Questions

Expert guidance regarding frame care, titanium durability, and prescription optics.

Use only lukewarm water and the pH-balanced STACKLY organic cleansing solution provided in your care kit. Never use hot water, window cleaners, or paper towels, which can strip anti-reflective coatings and dry out cellulose bio-acetate. Wipe gently with the supplied microfiber cloth from the bridge outwards.
Beta-titanium is forged in Sabae, Japan with an advanced molecular lattice that provides exceptional flexibility, 100% hypoallergenic immunity (zero nickel), and weights under 12 grams. It flexes under stress without deforming, ensuring permanent temple comfort behind the ear.
Your PD is the millimeter distance between your pupils. You can find it listed on your optometrist prescription, or schedule a 5-minute virtual video calibration with our master optician desk where our photogrammetry engine measures your exact visual axes to a tolerance of 0.1mm.
Every sunglass silhouette features distortion-free mineral glass or Japanese ultra-polar nylon lenses offering 100% UVA, UVB, and UVC protection up to 400nm, accompanied by an 8-layer backside anti-reflective coating to prevent bounce-back glare into your eyes.
As a registered client, you can bring or courier your eyewear to any STACKLY atelier worldwide at any time. We replace worn nose pads, re-torque custom German screws, ultrasonic-clean all components, and re-polish acetate temples completely free of charge for the life of the frame.
